This site belongs to Francisco Myers (fmyers.dev). This page explains what data it collects and what happens to it.

## What the contact form collects

Your name, email, project type, budget range (optional) and message. Nothing else is required.

## Where it goes

The form sends your message to a Google Apps Script that stores it in a private Google Sheet and notifies me by email. Only I have access. I use it to reply to you and to prepare a proposal, never for marketing lists, and I never sell or share it.

## How long I keep it

Up to 24 months after our last conversation. Then I delete it. If we work together, project records are kept as long as the law requires for invoicing.

## Spam protection

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person is sending it. Cloudflare processes technical signals from your browser for that check only.

## Analytics and cookies

The site uses cookieless analytics that count visits in aggregate. It does not identify you or follow you across sites. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Your theme and language choice is saved in your own browser (localStorage) and never sent to me.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address) for security and operation.

Write to {{email}} to see, correct or delete the data I hold about you. I reply within 2 business days.
